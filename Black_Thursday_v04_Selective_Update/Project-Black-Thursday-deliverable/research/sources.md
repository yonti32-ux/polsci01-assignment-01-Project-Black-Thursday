## Key academic boundaries

- The film, diagrammatic share traces, reservoir and 12-period Python model are **illustrations**, not records of historical stock-market series, banks' books, actual fiscal transfers or archival video.
- In 1983 it was **shares of affected banks** under pressure; do not describe that as all deposits becoming worthless or physical banks collapsing.
- **Share ownership** is distinct from day-to-day operational **management**.
- Closing the exchange in October 1983 and the stabilization plan of July 1985 are different episodes.
- Exact reopening day is described differently around **24/25 October**; deck uses **18 days** / late October.
- Material interpretations and conclusions need Yonatan and Liron's final editorial sign-off.
