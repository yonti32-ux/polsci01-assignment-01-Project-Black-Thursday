## Data and estimate discipline

**S01a estimates are not interchangeable.** Its executive summary reports about **$6.9 billion** as peak utilization/program value, while its abstract cites **$9.2 billion** cumulative purchases through 1991. S02 reports about **$9.1 billion in expenditure through Oct 1991 including financing cost to that date** and explicitly states that *net state loss* requires subtracting proceeds from later share sales. Different measures, periods, currency bases and financing treatment explain why these figures must not be reduced to a single unqualified 'cost' or 'loss'. If presenting one number, use S02's qualified wording and add a methodology note. **Never label a gross expenditure figure the net taxpayer loss.**

**Historical market data versus model data:** any price chart for 1983 must contain traceable data and a unit/deflator. The proposed Python model uses **periods 1–12** and a **unitless stability index**, never calendar dates or invented historical prices.

**Bank crisis versus 1985 stabilization:** the banking arrangement was negotiated in October 1983; the macroeconomic stabilization plan was introduced in July **1985**. Separate slides, labels, and timelines accordingly.
