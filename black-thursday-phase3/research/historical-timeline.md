## The causal narrative (teach *mechanism*, not a one-person blame story)

1. **Background:** Israeli inflation and depreciation of the shekel created a challenging financial context in the late 1970s and early 1980s. Shares in the major banks were perceived as unusually stable and attractive. [S01, S05]
2. **Multi-year practice:** Large commercial banks and related companies purchased bank shares and influenced purchasing through affiliated mutual funds, credit arrangements and brokerage channels. This **ויסות** helped support share prices despite underlying market conditions. [S01a, S02, S03]
3. **Institutional incentives:** Banks sought access to capital and benefited from sustained share prices; the practice produced conflicts of interest. Public authorities had knowledge or oversight responsibilities; exact institutional culpability should be attached to official findings rather than assumed. [S01a, S02, S04]
4. **Stress in 1983:** An earlier decline in nonbank shares and growing currency concerns encouraged shareholders to sell bank shares and shift toward foreign-currency assets. Supporting the market required ever-larger purchases by banks, and was not sustainable indefinitely. [S01a, S03]
5. **October shutdown and response:** On 6 October the stock exchange suspended operations amid severe share selling. Authorities and participating banks worked out an arrangement during the closure, involving future rights of eligible shareholders and eventual state ownership of substantial bank equity, **not** an immediate 1983 cash payout of every share at the published future-total cost. [S01, S01a, S02, S04]
6. **Long tail:** Purchases, ownership transitions, investigations and institutional reforms unfolded across subsequent years. Government rescue and managerial accountability were not the same policy decision nor completed on the same date. [S01a, S02, S06]

## What not to confuse

- **מניה ≠ פיקדון בנקאי:** the shock initially centered on shares. A potential run on bank deposits is a risk described in the legal retrospective, not a claim that all deposits were actually lost. [S04]
- **ויסות ≠ תעודת ביטוח אמיתית:** bank support gave an appearance of unusually safe investments but cannot be depicted as a universal legally valid guarantee to every holder.
- **גירעון/אינפלציה ≠ ויסות:** macro instability was context and a selling-pressure driver; manipulation was an ongoing banking/market practice with institutional incentives.
- **הסדר 1983 ≠ תכנית הייצוב 1985:** two separate policy interventions. [S05]
- **הוצאה ≠ הפסד:** $9.1b spent by 1991 incl. financing is not equal to a $9.1b net fiscal loss; public ultimately acquired shares which had resale value. [S02]
- **הלאמה/בעלות ≠ ניהול בפועל:** considerable state share ownership developed, but ordinary bank management was initially kept independent from direct governmental management. [S01a]
- **חקירה ≠ הרשעה:** distinguish state comptroller, commission recommendations, indictments, rulings and appeals. [S04; S06]
