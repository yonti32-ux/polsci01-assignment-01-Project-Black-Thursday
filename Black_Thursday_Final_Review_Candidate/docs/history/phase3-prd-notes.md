# PRD v1.0 → Phase 3 Editorial Decisions (proposed change control)

**Purpose:** Preserve the Phase-2 `PRD.md` as originally approved; document precise amendments for v1.1 rather than silently rewriting the requirement baseline.

## Decisions to carry into next PRD revision

1. **Keep Direction C:** 14-slide Hebrew-RTL documentary with embedded genuinely 3D video and Python-based interactive hypothetical simulation.
2. **Research question retained:** Explain the institutional incentives, oversight failures, investor responses and how government intervention distributed risk and responsibility.
3. **Historical factual scope:** 1970s/early 1980s share-support mechanism → 1983 selling pressure → **6 October** TASE closure → October arrangement → future redemption → 1986 inquiry and longer-term consequences.
4. **Careful leadership portrayal:** Correct **Yoram Aridor = Minister of Finance**. His resignation on **13 October 1983** followed controversy around the separate dollarization proposal. Do not script him as single author of a system-wide practice predating that month.
5. **Cost labels:** Present the State Comptroller's **~$9.1bn cumulative expenditure including financing through October 1991**, if a single figure is wanted; never call it the state's net ultimate loss. Yale's $6.9bn and $9.2bn are alternate accounting/program measures, not plug-in replacements.
6. **October reopening uncertainty:** Prefer 'לאחר 18 ימים' in main presentation. The specific 24 Oct operational date appears at Yale and an institutional historical timeline, but JTA contemporary headlines may reflect a 25 Oct reopening and should be reconciled with TASE primary records before hard-coding the date.
7. **Consequences:** Legal findings, commission recommendations, indictments and appeals are different stages. Date the Bejski report **16 April 1986**; never imply its recommendations were criminal sentences.
8. **Macro distinction:** 1985 stabilization is context, not the October 1983 bank-share arrangement. The **445% annual inflation figure belongs to 1984**.
9. **Data visualization:** Use verified historical numbers with source/unit/base-year or clearly mark figures as synthetic. The 3D movie is a mechanistic **illustration**; Python simulation results are **hypothetical**. No graphics should misleadingly resemble a real historical stock-price series.
10. **Assets:** Do not embed media copied from paywalled/copyrighted retrospective articles until licensed or appropriately cleared. Original visuals are the default.
