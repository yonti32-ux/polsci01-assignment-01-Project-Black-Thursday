# Original First-Trial Prompt — Preserved for Process Evidence

**Origin:** user-supplied in the initial project-planning chat (verbatim other than standardizing line breaks). This is evidence of an early idea, **not** a research-verified description.

> Make me a 3D video in which Yoram Aridor, the Israeli Minister of Economy, causes the crisis of bank shares in 1983. Make the video like an information video about how the stock crisis happened, what were the stages to this economic collapse according to the information given in the link: [https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/משבר_מניות_הבנקים](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/משבר_מניות_הבנקים)

## Lessons learned during research (not part of original prompt)
- Incorrect role: Aridor was **Minister of Finance**, not of Economy.
- Unsupported single-actor causation: a multi-year institutional share-price-support practice is not plausibly attributable solely to him.
- Wikipedia is not enough for contested historical interpretation.
- '3D video' alone does not satisfy the assignment: it also needs a navigable **HTML presentation**, genuine **Python** participation, a **PRD** and documented GitHub development.
